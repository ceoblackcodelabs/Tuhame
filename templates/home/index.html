How {{ site_name }} works

Three roles, one platform.

1

Renters find & move

Search verified listings by area or on the map, save favourites, book viewings, and hire a mover when you are ready.

2

Landlords list & add tenants

List for free, then add each tenant to a unit. A rent bill is created automatically and recurs every month.

3

Tenants pay, you confirm

Tenants see only their own bills and report payment. You confirm it, and your dashboard and reports update instantly.

Rent management built in

Everything a landlord needs to collect rent and keep records — no spreadsheets.

Tenant roster

See who lives where and who has paid this month at a glance.

Automatic monthly bills

Rent, water, service charge — recurring bills are generated for you, including any months you missed.

Overdue tracking

Late bills flip to overdue on their own so you know exactly who to follow up.

Payment confirmation

A tenant’s payment is only marked paid once you confirm it — no false “paid”.

Dashboard & reports

Money collected by day, month and method, per property, with daily and custom reports.

Private tenant portal

Each tenant sees only their own bills, receipts and history.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free for landlords?

Listing your property is free. Rent management tools are available from your landlord dashboard.

How do tenants pay rent?

Tenants pay you directly, then report the payment in their portal. The bill shows as awaiting confirmation until you confirm it.

Can a tenant see other tenants’ bills?

No. Each tenant only sees their own bills and payments.

How does a tenant get access?

They create a Tuhame account. You then add them to your property using their email, and their bills appear in their profile.

Are listings verified?

Owners go through a verification step before they get landlord tools. Contact support if something looks wrong.
